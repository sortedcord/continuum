title: Phone Screen Mirroring
description: Low-latency, interactive Android screen mirroring and remote control on your Linux desktop.
---

Continuum Screen Mirroring brings your complete phone display onto your Linux desktop in an ultra-responsive, interactive window, allowing you to use all your Android apps without ever taking your eyes off your computer monitor.

## User Experience

### 1. Interactive Desktop Phone Window
* Launching Screen Mirroring opens a crisp, scalable desktop window mirroring your Android screen in real time.
* **Full Remote Input**:
  * **Mouse & Touchpad**: Left-click to tap, click-and-drag to swipe, two-finger scroll to flick through feeds and web pages.
  * **Keyboard Typing**: Use your physical desktop mechanical keyboard to type text, emojis, and search queries into phone apps at full typing speed.
  * **System Shortcuts**: Standard navigation hotkeys (e.g. `Esc` or right-click for Back, `Super+H` for Home, `Super+Tab` for App Switcher).
* **Audio Forwarding**: All audio generated by the phone streams seamlessly through your desktop speakers or headphones with frame-accurate lip-sync.

### 2. Privacy & Power-Saving Lock
* While the screen is being mirrored on your desktop, the physical phone screen remains dark or displays a subtle "Continuum Active" ambient lock screen.
* Passersby cannot view what you are doing on your phone, and your phone's battery and OLED display are preserved from overheating and burn-in.
* If you pick up the phone physically and unlock it, desktop mirroring can automatically pause or yield control gracefully.

## Continuum Augmentations (Beyond Apple)

* **Multi-Window Independent App Streaming**:
  * *Apple's Limitation*: macOS Sequoia iPhone Mirroring forces you to view the entire phone in a single vertical phone-shaped frame.
  * *Continuum Superpower*: Continuum can isolate individual Android apps into **separate, native floating desktop windows**. Open your banking app in one window, a messaging app in another, and a mobile game in a third, arranging and resizing them freely across your Linux desktop workspaces.
* **Custom Aspect Ratios & DPI Scaling**: Adjust the virtual DPI scaling of the phone window to fit large high-DPI 4K Linux monitors comfortably.
* **Clipboard Isolation Toggle**: Choose whether the mirrored phone window shares the desktop clipboard or operates in an isolated sandbox.
