Continuum Sidecar turns your Android tablet or phone into an extended desktop display for your Linux workstation, as well as a high-precision graphics drawing tablet with active stylus support.

## User Experience

### 1. Wireless Second Monitor
* When working at your desk, a coffee shop, or traveling:
  * Select your Android tablet from the Continuum display settings.
  * Your desktop immediately extends onto the tablet screen as a true second monitor.
  * Drag windows, toolbars, terminal panes, browser tabs, or reference documents seamlessly onto the tablet screen.
* Supports both **Extended Display** (additional workspace) and **Mirrored Display** (mirroring your primary screen for presentations).
* Smooth, high-refresh-rate rendering (up to 60fps or 120fps on capable tablet screens) over local Wi-Fi or zero-latency USB cable.

### 2. Creative Graphics Tablet Mode
* For artists, designers, and note-takers:
  * Open creative software on Linux (Krita, GIMP, Blender, Inkscape, or Figma).
  * Move the canvas window to your Android tablet.
  * Draw directly on the tablet screen using an active stylus (Samsung S-Pen, Lenovo Precision Pen, or universal USI styluses).
  * Full support for **pressure sensitivity**, **tilt angle**, and palm rejection.

---

## Continuum Augmentations (Beyond Apple)

* **Unlimited Virtual Displays**: Unlike Apple Sidecar (which is strictly 1 Mac to 1 iPad), Continuum allows you to extend your desktop across multiple Android tablets simultaneously (e.g. tablet on the left for chat, tablet on the right for documentation).
* **Hardware Agnostic**: Works with virtually any Android tablet or large-screen phone running modern Android, not just high-end premium models.
* **Custom Aspect Ratios & Resolutions**: Match the native aspect ratio (16:10, 4:3, 3:2) and resolution of your tablet display perfectly without black letterboxing or blurry scaling.
* **Touch-Screen Shortcut Bars**: Display customizable on-screen shortcut buttons (Undo, Redo, Brush Size, Color Picker) along the edge of the tablet display while drawing.
