title: Continuity Sketch & Markup
description: Instant drawing, handwriting, and PDF signing on your mobile device with live desktop synchronization.
---

Continuity Sketch and Markup allows you to instantly invoke your phone or tablet's touchscreen and stylus to draw diagrams, write handwritten notes, or sign PDF contracts, with the results updating in real time on your Linux desktop.

## User Experience

### 1. Instant Document Sketching
* Working on a document, markdown note, or slide deck on Linux:
  * Select **"Insert Sketch from Phone"** from the application or system tray.
  * Your Android phone or tablet screen immediately lights up with a full-screen, responsive drawing canvas.
* As you draw or write with your finger or stylus, your strokes appear in real time inside your Linux document.
* Tapping **Done** on your phone finalizes the sketch and inserts a clean vector (SVG) or high-resolution PNG image directly into your active document.

### 2. PDF & Document Markup
* When reviewing an architectural drawing, PDF contract, or image on Linux:
  * Click **"Markup on Android"**.
  * The document loads on your Android tablet/phone with standard annotation tools: highlighters, pens, callout arrows, text boxes, and signature stamps.
  * Annotate, circle revisions, or sign your name with a stylus.
  * Every annotation syncs back immediately to your Linux document viewer or editor.

---

## Continuum Augmentations (Beyond Apple)

* **Direct Vector (SVG) Output**: While Apple produces rasterized bitmaps for many markup tasks, Continuum prioritizes resolution-independent vector SVG exports, allowing infinite scalability in desktop publishing tools (Inkscape, Scribus).
* **Saved Reusable Signatures**: Store your cryptographic or handwritten signature securely in the local Android keystore to sign incoming Linux documents with a single fingerprint confirmation.
* **Canvas Grid & Template Options**: Choose graph paper, dot-grid, isometric grids, or music staves directly on your tablet canvas during live desktop sketch sessions.
