Continuum turns your Android phone into an emergency or everyday input peripheral for your Linux laptop or desktop. Whether your physical mouse died, you are lounging on a sofa watching a movie, delivering a presentation, or playing a game, your phone becomes a versatile multi-function input device.

## User Experience

### 1. Multi-Touch Precision Trackpad
* Open the Continuum Input mode on your phone to turn the entire screen into a smooth, low-latency glass touchpad:
  * **1-Finger Tap**: Left click.
  * **2-Finger Tap**: Right click (context menu).
  * **2-Finger Drag / Flick**: Smooth vertical and horizontal kinetic scrolling.
  * **Pinch to Zoom**: Zoom in/out on web pages, photos, and documents.
  * **3-Finger Swipe**: Switch between virtual Linux desktop workspaces.
  * **Haptic Click Feedback**: Your phone’s vibration motor pulses to give tactile feedback on every click and drag event.

### 2. Remote Keyboard, Numpad & Voice Dictation
* **Full Mobile Typing**: Tap into any desktop text box, document, or terminal and type using your preferred mobile keyboard (Gboard, SwiftKey, etc.).
* **Voice-to-Text Dictation**: Tap the microphone icon on your mobile keyboard to dictate long paragraphs of speech—your phone transcribes your speech in real time and sends the text straight into your Linux active window.
* **Specialized Keypads**:
  * **Full Numeric Keypad (Numpad)**: Instant numpad for laptops that lack physical number pads.
  * **Function & Navigation Keys**: Quick buttons for `Esc`, `Tab`, `F1-F12`, `Home`, `End`, `Page Up/Down`, and arrow keys.
  * **Media Remote**: Big buttons for Play/Pause, Next Track, Volume Up/Down, and Mute.

### 3. Virtual Gamepad & Gyro Controller
* Turn your phone into a wireless game controller for native Linux games, Steam, and emulators:
  * **Dual Virtual Analog Sticks**: Responsive touch thumbsticks for movement and camera look.
  * **Action Buttons**: D-Pad, ABXY face buttons, and L1/L2/R1/R2 shoulder triggers.
  * **Motion / Gyroscope Steering**: Tilt and steer your phone like a physical steering wheel in racing games or use gyro aiming in shooters.
  * **Custom Game Layouts**: Save custom button mappings for different games or retro consoles.

### 4. Gyro Air Mouse & Presentation Clicker
* Delivering a presentation or controlling media from across the room:
  * Hold your phone like a TV remote or laser pointer.
  * Wave the phone through the air—the on-screen desktop cursor moves in lockstep using the phone’s internal gyroscope and accelerometer sensors.
  * Large thumb buttons for **Next Slide**, **Previous Slide**, and **Laser Pointer Spotlight**.

## Continuum Augmentations (Beyond Apple)

* **Apple Ecosystem Comparison**: Apple has no native feature allowing an iPhone to function as a general-purpose Mac trackpad, keyboard, or gamepad without third-party paid App Store utilities. Continuum builds this directly into the foundational core.
* **Customizable Macro Pad (Elgato Stream Deck Alternative)**: Configure custom touch grids on your phone with custom labels and colors to trigger desktop hotkeys, launch terminal scripts, switch OBS scenes, or mute microphone.
* **Multi-Player Virtual Gamepads**: Connect multiple phones simultaneously to one Linux machine (e.g., 4 phones acting as 4 gamepads) for local split-screen multiplayer gaming with friends.
* **Zero-Driver Linux Input (`uinput`)**: The Linux desktop sees the phone as a true hardware USB/Bluetooth mouse, keyboard, or joystick, ensuring compatibility across all desktop environments and games without specialized drivers.
