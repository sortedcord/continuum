Continuity Camera allows you to use your Android phone’s advanced camera sensors and image signal processor as a wireless, ultra-high-quality webcam for your Linux laptop or desktop.

## User Experience

### 1. Zero-Setup Wireless Webcam
* When you open any video conferencing app on Linux (Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Discord, OBS Studio, or WebRTC in a browser):
  * Your Android phone appears as a standard video input device (e.g. *"Continuum Camera (Pixel 9)"*).
  * Selecting it wakes the phone's camera stream wirelessly with imperceptible latency.
  * Your phone’s screen stays locked or displays a discreet, low-power preview to conserve battery and screen wear.

### 2. Desktop Camera Controls
A lightweight desktop floating palette allows you to adjust camera parameters on the fly without touching your phone:
* **Lens Switching**: Toggle between the Main wide lens, Ultra-Wide lens, and Telephoto lens.
* **Resolution & Framerate**: Select between 1080p60, 4K30, or battery-saving 720p modes.
* **Flashlight / Torch**: Turn on the phone's rear LED flash as a fill light for low-light environments.
* **Exposure & Focus Locking**: Click-to-focus on specific objects or lock manual exposure.

### 3. Instant Document Scanner & Photo Trigger
* Inside document editors or messaging apps on Linux, choose **"Take Photo with Phone"** or **"Scan Document with Phone"**.
* Your phone's screen immediately illuminates with a camera capture viewfinder.
* Point the camera at a physical receipt, document, or object and snap the photo:
  * In Document Mode, the phone automatically detects paper edges, flattens perspective distortion, adjusts contrast, and converts it into a clean, searchable PDF.
  * The resulting PDF or photo instantly inserts into your desktop document or active folder.

## Continuum Augmentations (Beyond Apple)

* **Multi-Camera Simultaneous Streaming**: Stream both the rear camera and the front selfie camera simultaneously to create an instant picture-in-picture streaming setup in OBS or presentation tools.
* **Raw Video Passthrough**: Capture high-bitrate raw video streams for professional desktop post-processing, avoiding aggressive proprietary color filtering.
* **Custom On-Device AI Framing**: Open-source face-tracking (Center Stage equivalent) that operates entirely on-device, keeping you centered in frame as you move around your desk.
* **Virtual Backgrounds & Effects**: Support for local background blur, bokeh effects, and green-screen removal performed on the phone's GPU before streaming to desktop, saving laptop CPU/battery.
