---
title: Media Cast Receiver
description: Turn your Linux computer into a high-performance wireless audio and video receiver.
---

Continuum allows your Linux computer to act as a wireless media cast receiver (equivalent to Apple AirPlay or Google Cast), allowing you to project videos, music, photos, and presentations directly from your phone to your computer's monitor and speakers.

## User Experience

### 1. Wireless Video & Audio Casting
* While watching a video or listening to audio on your Android device (in YouTube, Spotify, VLC, gallery apps, or a mobile browser), tap the **Cast** icon.
* Your Linux PC appears in the receiver list (e.g. *"Living Room Desktop"* or *"Work Laptop"*).
* Selecting it immediately streams the media to your Linux desktop:
  * Plays video smoothly in a borderless window or full-screen player.
  * Routes audio through your high-quality desktop speakers, soundbar, or studio monitors.

### 2. Full Media Controls
* Control playback directly from your phone (play, pause, scrub timeline, volume) or use your Linux media hotkeys and desktop sound widgets.
* Metadata sync: artist name, album art, track duration, and lyrics display automatically in the Linux media player widget.

---

## Continuum Augmentations (Beyond Apple)

* **OBS Studio & Streaming Integration**: Treat incoming media streams as an independent video capture source inside OBS Studio for live reaction streams, game streaming, or webinars.
* **Multi-Room Audio Broadcasting**: Cast audio from your phone to multiple Linux machines and speakers around your home simultaneously in synchronized lockstep.
* **Stream Recording**: Record incoming cast video or audio streams locally to an MP4/MKV file with a single desktop button for archiving presentations or lectures.
