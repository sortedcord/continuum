Continuity Microphone transforms your phone’s high-quality multi-microphone array into a wireless desktop microphone input for calls, podcasts, voice recordings, and streaming.

## User Experience

### 1. Studio-Quality Wireless Audio
* Modern smartphones feature advanced multi-microphone arrays designed for beamforming, wind reduction, and noise suppression.
* Continuity Microphone exposes your phone as an active audio input source on your Linux desktop.
* In your Linux sound settings or recording software (Audacity, OBS Studio, Discord, Zoom), choose *"Continuum Wireless Mic"* as your input device.

### 2. Intelligent Audio Modes
* **Voice Isolation**: Emphasizes speech frequencies while suppressing ambient room noise (air conditioning, computer fans, keyboard clatter).
* **Wide Spectrum**: Captures raw acoustic ambiance, ideal for musical instruments, interviews, or acoustic environments.
* **Mute Sync**: Tapping the mute button in your Linux call app or desktop status bar synchronizes the mute state with the phone's hardware mic, illuminated by a visible status indicator.

---

## Continuum Augmentations (Beyond Apple)

* **Independent Wireless Mic (Without Video)**: Use your phone purely as a high-grade wireless lapel or desk microphone during desktop gaming or podcasting without keeping the camera sensor running.
* **Low-Latency Opus Compression**: Tuned for ultra-low packet latency over local Wi-Fi, preventing voice lag during real-time multiplayer gaming or collaborative meetings.
* **Desktop Soundboard / Pass-through**: Route selected desktop audio back through the phone for monitoring or dual-device podcast co-hosting.
